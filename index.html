04About

Hello, I'm Serene.

I'm a Hong Kong–based graphic designer, video editor and photographer. I currently work at Hong Kong Baptist University, where I produce promotional and instructional videos, event photography and social media content, and prepare reporting material for programme teams.

Before that I spent three years at Hong Kong Metropolitan University supporting academic programmes, designing exhibition panels, information graphics and campaign visuals along the way. That mix shaped how I work: I like a brief that needs both a clear structure and a strong visual idea, and I'm comfortable taking it from sketch to print-ready artwork.

Outside of work I shoot portraits and sport, and I keep practising 3D, motion graphics and AI-assisted workflows so the tools never get in the way of the idea.
